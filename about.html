About The Attending Playbook

Financial clarity for the attending transition.

Practical financial education for residents, fellows, and new attending physicians.

After years of medical training, physicians face a wave of important financial decisions, often all at once. Student loans, employment contracts, insurance, retirement benefits, taxes, and investing suddenly become immediate priorities.

The Attending Playbook exists to make those decisions easier to understand, helping physicians approach the transition into attending practice with greater clarity and confidence.

Why this exists

Financial education for the attending transition.

The transition from residency or fellowship into attending practice is one of the most significant financial turning points in a physician's career.

A substantial increase in income brings new opportunities, but also new complexity. Decisions about student loan repayment, retirement accounts, employee benefits, disability and life insurance, taxes, investing, and homeownership can have lasting financial consequences.

The Attending Playbook brings these topics together in one accessible educational resource. The goal is to help physicians understand their options, recognize important tradeoffs, and ask better questions before making major financial decisions.

Residents & fellows Preparing for contracts, student loan decisions, insurance, and the transition out of training.
New attendings Building a plan for cash flow, benefits, investing, taxes, housing, and competing financial goals.
Residency programs Practical transition-to-practice education built around the financial decisions trainees actually face.
