Who’s behind the playbook

Built by a financial planner who works with physicians.

I’m Zac Otwell, CFP®. I created The Attending Playbook to make the financial transition from training to attending life easier to navigate — especially the decisions around student loans, benefits, insurance, taxes, investing, and the first big jump in income.

The goal here is simple: practical education you can use before a financial decision becomes urgent.
